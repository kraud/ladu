Do and do not

The short version. The full rules are in 01-principles.md to 07-agent-checklist.md.

✓ Do

  • Use tokens: var(--accent), not a hex.
  • Reuse Button, .card, .chip, .dtable, SidebarLayout.
  • Show every language with a flag and a code, in the account's order.
  • Pair colour with an icon or text for state.
  • Give every page loading, empty, error and success states.
  • Test light, dark, 375px, 1440px, and German or Estonian text.
  • Put all text in the i18n files for five languages.

✕ Do not

  • Add a gradient, a heavy shadow or an emoji icon.
  • Colour a whole card with a language colour.
  • Treat EN or ES as the main language.
  • Use serif for body text, buttons or labels.
  • Use --accent for small text. Use --accent-strong.
  • Write a second table, dialog, menu or side panel.
  • Copy a pattern from a "Known drift" note.
