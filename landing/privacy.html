Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-24.

Ladu is a vocabulary manager built and run by a single developer. This page describes what information Ladu collects, why, and how you can have it removed.

What we collect

Account information. Your name, username, email address, and the languages you tell us you speak. If you sign up with a password, we store a salted hash of it — never the password itself. If you sign in with Google, we store your Google account's unique identifier and the email address Google confirms for it, instead of a password.

Your vocabulary data. The words, translations, and grammatical details you add, and your practice history — this is what powers the app's spaced-repetition exercises and stays tied to your account.

Basic technical data. Error reports (via Sentry) when something breaks, so it can be fixed — these can include the page you were on and what went wrong, not your vocabulary content.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your data to anyone. We don't run advertising, and we don't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics to profile you across other sites.

Who else sees it

A short list, each used only for the specific job named:

  • Google — only if you choose "Sign in with Google," to verify your identity.
  • Resend — to deliver account emails (verification, notifications).
  • Sentry — to report application errors, as described above.
  • Cloudflare — sits in front of our server for security and performance; it can see traffic metadata, not your account data.

Cookies

Ladu uses one short-lived cookie during Google sign-in, to safely complete that login — it expires within minutes either way. We don't use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can ask for your account and all associated data to be deleted at any time by contacting us at the address below — we'll confirm once it's done.

Children's privacy

Ladu is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
