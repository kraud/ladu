Welcome, and thank you for stopping by.

Ladu is a vocabulary manager for people who speak more than one language. You save
each word once, with its translations in every language you know. Then you practice
them together.

This blog is where we will share news and small updates about the project: what is
new, what we are working on, and what comes next.

There is no newsletter and no tracking. To follow along, you can use the
[Atom feed](/blog/feed.xml) in your feed reader, or come back to this page.

Stay tuned for news.
